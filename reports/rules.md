# Lint rules

Generated by `aip report`.

| Rule | Severity | What it checks | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AIP001` | high | Policy is well formed | Levels, phases and kinds that do not add up make every plan wrong. |
| `AIP002` | high | Playbooks are well formed | A step with an unknown phase or no deadline silently drops out of the plan. |
| `AIP003` | high | Step roles and prerequisites are valid and acyclic | A step nobody owns, or that waits for itself, never gets done. |
| `AIP004` | high | Playbooks contain, and review | A playbook that does not stop the harm or learn from it is a diary. |
| `AIP005` | high | Containment is as fast as the policy requires | Slow containment at the top severities is the cost the policy exists to cap. |
| `AIP006` | high | Every role has a backup | People are unavailable; a role without cover stalls the incident. |
| `AIP007` | high | Every needed role can be filled | A role nobody holds is a step nobody does. |
| `AIP008` | high | Every incident kind has a playbook | An incident with no playbook is improvised. |
| `AIP009` | high | Obligations are well formed | A notification clock with a bad start or length is wrong at the worst time. |
| `AIP010` | high | Legal deadlines are reviewed or marked placeholders | A deadline nobody reviewed is a guess that looks like a requirement. |
| `AIP011` | high | Obligations and the steps that satisfy them match | An obligation no step satisfies is an alarm that can never be cleared. |
| `AIP012` | high | Incident records are well formed | A record with wrong references cannot be planned or audited. |
| `AIP013` | high | Steps are done by the right person, in order | An action by someone without the role, or before its prerequisite, is not the action the playbook meant. |
| `AIP014` | high | Severity is not understated | Declaring a lower severity than the facts give hides the incident from the response it needs. |
| `AIP015` | high | Nothing open is overdue | An overdue notification or containment step is the finding that matters most. |
| `AIP016` | high | Closed incidents are complete | Closing with steps or notifications outstanding hides unfinished work. |
