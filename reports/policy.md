An incident is run at the most severe level of what was declared and what any rule below gives.

## Severity rules

| Level | When | Because |
|---|---|---|
| `sev1` | harm in physical | Physical harm is possible |
| `sev1` | data exposed in personal, confidential; ongoing = true | Personal or confidential data is exposed and the exposure is still happening |
| `sev1` | autonomous action = true; harm in financial, physical | An agent took actions of its own and money or people were harmed |
| `sev2` | data exposed in personal, confidential | Personal or confidential data was exposed |
| `sev2` | users affected at least 1000 | A large number of people are affected |
| `sev2` | autonomous action = true | An agent took actions of its own |
| `sev2` | harm in financial | Financial harm occurred |
| `sev3` | customer facing = true; harm in reputational | Customers saw the problem and it caused reputational harm |
| `sev3` | data exposed in internal | Internal data was exposed |
